## Key Editing & Sound Design Notes for This Track

* **The `0:14.0` and `0:48.0` Breaths Are Your Anchor Transitions:** The track naturally gives you two dramatic drumless pauses right before `0:15.2` and `0:49.6`. Use `0:14.0–0:15.2` to pull the camera back from the 3D flyover into the first TUI feature cut, and use `0:48.0–0:49.6` to wind up the 3D morph from the terminal TUI into the NSH Web and macOS apps.


* **Syncing the Rapid-Fire Montage (`0:41.0 – 0:48.0`):** During this 7-second build, the snare drum hits every ~0.55 seconds (quarter notes) and doubles up at `0:45.5`. Changing the feature card every 2 snare hits (`1.1s`) for the first four features, then every single snare hit (`0.55s`) for the final two features, will make the visual pacing feel locked to the rising tension.


* **Audio Ducking for the iOS Segment (`1:06.8 – 1:21.5`):** Because `0:49.6 – 1:23.8` is the loudest, most compressed section of the track, apply a clean sidechain/volume duck (around `-8dB` to `-10dB` with a gentle high-shelf cut around `3kHz`) from `1:06.8` to `1:20.0` so the scripted human voice and GPT-4o live voice response cut through effortlessly, then bring the track back to `0dB` at `1:20.5` for the final impact hit at `1:23.8`.
